# Website audit — 1 October 2026

## Fixes

- Reproduced a production media-delivery bug: valid suffix byte ranges returned 416, while reversed and out-of-file ranges reached R2 and became 500 errors.
- Normalize and validate ranges before requesting storage. Support suffix ranges, open-ended ranges, and oversized end offsets. Invalid ranges now return 416 with the total file size. The shared handler covers public, student, and teacher previews without changing access rules.
- Updated Multer to 2.4.0 and ip-address to 10.7.2 for published security fixes. The lockfile audit reports zero known vulnerabilities.

## Verification before deployment

- 58 automated tests passed, including new range regressions on teacher and student routes, authentication, permissions, registration/groups, assignments, attendance, upload validation, Google/OTP adapters, and genuine XLSX exports.
- The isolated 200-student classroom simulation passed as part of the suite. This uses local SQLite, local test OTPs and local media; it does not establish production capacity.
- TypeScript and the production build passed.
- Browser checks covered all ten teacher navigation pages and all eight student navigation pages with isolated fixture accounts; no console errors were recorded.
- Created and closed a disposable local attendance session, verified its analysis entry and student-table preview, and confirmed it disappeared from student attendance.
- Inspected the student overview at phone and tablet sizes (390 × 844 and 820 × 1180 viewport settings): no horizontal overflow, posters and cards remained visible.
- Checked the live public homepage, signup entry, guest subject/folder navigation, and public PDF links without modifying production records.

## Scope limits

Real email/SMS delivery, Google-provider interaction, physical-device location permission prompts and real GPS accuracy were not exercised. Provider adapters and attendance validation were tested in isolation. Responsive browser checks are not physical-device certification. No production student accounts, attendance records, publication settings or paid plans were changed.
